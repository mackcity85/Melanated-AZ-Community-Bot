How to Play

  1. Choose 2–6 players and start.
  2. Take turns rolling the dice and moving.
  3. 🍬 Treat: collect one; three treats earn a bonus space.
  4. 🎯 Challenge: complete a fun challenge or move back one.
  5. 👑 Kingdom: collect a treat and move one extra space.
  6. 🕸️ Trap: the next player loses a turn.
  7. Reach the Chocolate Castle first to win!

Shared-screen party mode: pass the device between players.
